Quick test: glowing light
  1. Add one battery, switch, resistor and LED or bulb.
  2. Turn Connect parts on.
  3. Tap in this order: battery → switch → resistor → light → battery.
  4. Tap Run. Turn the switch on to light it.

Wires attach to visible terminal locations. The simulator checks a simple closed loop; it is not a real-world circuit safety checker.

How to use the bench

  • Orbit: drag empty space.
  • Select: tap a part.
  • Move: turn Move part on and drag; compatible parts snap together when close.
  • Connect: tap two parts. Compatible mechanical parts auto-align; electrical links snap to component terminals.
  • Save: stores this project in this browser on this device.
