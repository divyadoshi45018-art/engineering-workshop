Quick test: glowing light
  1. Add one battery, switch, resistor and LED or bulb.
  2. Turn Connect parts on.
  3. Connect terminal-to-terminal: battery → switch → resistor → light → battery. Add a wire and drag an endpoint near a terminal to snap-connect.
  4. Tap Run. Turn the switch on to light it.

Wire endpoints snap to nearby terminals. The simulator follows connected paths through wires and components; this is educational, not a real-world circuit safety checker.

How to use the bench

  • Orbit: drag empty space.
  • Select: tap a part.
  • Move: turn Move part on and drag; compatible parts snap together when close.
  • Connect: tap two parts in Connect mode, or drag a wire endpoint close to a terminal for automatic snapping. Compatible mechanical parts auto-align.
  • Save: stores this project in this browser on this device.
